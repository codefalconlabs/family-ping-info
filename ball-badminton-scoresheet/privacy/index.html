Privacy Policy

Ball Badminton Scoresheet

Effective date November 27, 2026

Code Falcon Labs provides Ball Badminton Scoresheet for Android. This policy explains how the app handles information when you record matches, manage teams, back up records, or share scoresheets.

Information stored on your device

The app stores what you enter in its private storage on your device. This can include team, player, coach, and manager names, player chest numbers, tournament and venue details, and match events such as points, service turns, substitutions, and results. The app uses this information to score matches, reuse team rosters, and prepare scoresheets.

The app has no account sign-in. Code Falcon Labs does not receive the records you enter through the app. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Backups, sharing, and external links

When you create a backup, Android lets you choose where to save the file. A backup can include games, teams, and player records. If you choose a cloud storage provider such as Google Drive, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. To restore a backup, you select the file to import.

If you share a PDF scoresheet, the app passes the file to the recipient or app you select. The PDF may include player names, chest numbers, and other details entered for the match. A recipient may keep a copy of the file.

The Rules and Regulations link opens the Ball Badminton Federation of India website in your browser. The website handles your visit according to its own practices.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved games in the app and edit saved team rosters. Edits to a saved roster do not change player details already recorded in a match. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app removes its local records. Manage backup files and exported PDFs separately in the storage or service where they were saved.
