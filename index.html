Privacy Policy

App: Family Ping

Developer: Code Falcon Labs

Effective date: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Family Ping collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information: username, mobile number, password credentials handled by Back4App/Parse authentication, and display name.
  • Trusted contact information: trusted contact names, mobile numbers, invite codes, pairing request status, and device identifiers generated for app functionality.
  • Location information: device location is collected only when a trusted contact requests location sharing and the app has the required location permissions.
  • Camera access: the camera is used only to scan QR invite codes. The app does not store photos, videos, or camera frames.
  • Device and notification information: Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens, installation identifiers, app device identifiers, notification status, app version, and timestamps needed to send pairing and location request notifications.
  • Technical information: logs, request status, error messages, and basic diagnostic data needed to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Information

We use information only for app functionality, including:

  • Creating and signing in to your account.
  • Creating QR invite codes and processing pairing requests.
  • Approving, rejecting, syncing, and removing trusted contacts.
  • Sending and receiving trusted-contact location requests.
  • Collecting and sharing location only with an approved trusted contact when a location request is made.
  • Sending push notifications for pairing, approval, recovery, and location request events.
  • Checking app version status and requiring updates when necessary for security or compatibility.
  • Maintaining service reliability, preventing abuse, and troubleshooting app issues.

Camera Permission

The app requests camera permission to scan QR invite codes for trusted-contact pairing. Camera data is processed on the device for QR scanning. We do not upload, sell, share, or store camera images or videos.

Location Permission

The app requests location permission, including background location where enabled by the user, so the phone can respond to location requests from approved trusted contacts. Location is not collected continuously for advertising or analytics. Location is collected when needed to reply to a trusted-contact request and is shared only with the approved requester.

Push Notifications

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging and Back4App/Parse to send notifications about trusted-contact pairing, approvals, password recovery approval, and location requests. Notification tokens and app installation identifiers are used only to deliver these app notifications.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate the app:

  • Back4App/Parse: account authentication, app database, remote configuration, trusted-contact records, invite records, and location request records.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notification delivery.
  • Google Maps or browser apps: when you choose to open a received location in a map.

These providers process information as needed to provide app functionality. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed for the app's user-facing features:

  • Your display name, mobile number, and pairing request details may be shown to the trusted contact you invite or request approval from.
  • Your location may be shared with an approved trusted contact after that contact sends a location request and your app responds.
  • Information may be processed by Back4App/Parse and Firebase Cloud Messaging to operate the app.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect users, the app, or the public from harm or abuse.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including encrypted network transport where supported by service providers, account authentication, and limiting data use to app functionality. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account, trusted-contact, invite, notification, and location request records for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or enforce our terms. You may remove trusted contacts in the app. To request account or data deletion, contact us using the privacy contact below.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users who are legally permitted to use location sharing and account-based services in their jurisdiction. The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke camera, notification, or location permissions in Android settings, but some app features may stop working.
  • You can remove trusted contacts from within the app.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The updated policy will be posted with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
